# Permission letters

Third-party tutorial clips need a written permission letter in this folder before they are used in a demo video or a submission.

No permission letters are included. The shipped recorded Samples contain third-party
stock footage and saved outputs, with source/licence links in
[CREDITS.md](../public/samples/CREDITS.md). They are not self-filmed placeholders.
Source credits alone do not establish compliance with the submission policy above.
Use an owned lesson for the primary filmed demonstration, and reconcile stock-asset
permission records before including that footage in a submission or demo video.
