## Name

Reel2Real

## One-liner

Put the phone on a stand, upload a short tutorial, and follow each step while SAM draws a box around the object.

## Problem

A tutorial video keeps playing while your hands are busy. It is easy to lose the object the step is about, and easy to move on before the frame actually matches. Pausing, scrubbing, and guessing which thing the narrator means breaks the lesson.

## What it does

Reel2Real is a browser coach. You upload a clip you filmed. Muse Spark (`muse-spark-1.3`) turns that clip into a short list of steps. On the live camera, SAM 3.1 (`sam-3.1`) draws a box around the object. Contour outlines appear only on the recorded Sample demos.

The browser does not hold the model key. The public app accepts a same-origin segment request, then sends it through a private, authenticated server-side forward to a worker service on the host's private network. The worker calls Meta SAM 3.1 and returns the result. If the forward or the Meta call fails, the coach shows Highlights unavailable and does not invent a box.

Five Sample demos ship with recorded SAM output: masala chai, flask colour, origami, seed planting, and latte art. Those outlines are labelled Sample · recorded SAM output. They are never labelled Live. The flask demo shows Grown-up supervision and does not name a chemical.

Some steps show no outline. The recorded masks failed verification (size and position checks), so the step shows a plain hint instead of a guessed outline. On current main that includes latte art steps 1 and 3, masala chai step 3, and origami step 3.

The landing hero stays on the chai demo. Every demo card credits the creator. Chai is credited to Pexels contributor.

## How it uses Meta models

| Model | Id | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Muse Spark | `muse-spark-1.3` | Once, when a clip is extracted into steps |
| SAM 3.1 | `sam-3.1` | Live coach keyframes. It draws a box around the object. Sample demos replay contour outlines recorded from this model |

Calls go to `POST https://api.meta.ai/v1/responses`. `/api/spark` extracts steps from an uploaded clip. `/api/segment` asks live SAM to draw a box around the object, through the private forward described above. Sample demos do not use that live path. They replay recorded contour outlines.

Muse Image is not used.

The default coach mode is keyframe stills. A short video chunk was measured slower than the live budget in this repo, so chunk tracking stays optional.

## Tech

Next.js app, Node 22, deployed as a web service. The prod URL is above. Steps are validated against a JSON schema. Sample scenes, crops, and credits are data in `src/samples/demos.ts` plus files under `public/samples/`. Preview pages can sit behind a password. Secrets stay out of the repo and out of this draft.
