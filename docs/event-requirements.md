## Deadline

Project submissions are due **November 4, 2026 at 9:00 PM ET**, which is **Thursday, November 5, 2026 at 07:30 IST (Asia/Calcutta)** and November 5 at 02:00 UTC. New York observes EST on the submission date; the event page's structured end date confirms the UTC conversion. The event page says dates may change and changes will be posted there.

The stated application deadline was October 25, or earlier if 20,000 acceptances were reached. The event page uses ET for that earlier cutoff while the rules use EST; that wording difference does not affect the November submission conversion. Judging is November 4 to 8; the event page lists November 9 for live finals and winners.

## Eligibility and track

- Every participant must have applied and been accepted, be at least 18 and the age of majority in their home jurisdiction, and meet the rules' geographic, sanctions, employment, government-affiliation and family/household restrictions. This repository does not establish the user's eligibility, acceptance or signed-waiver status.
- A participant and project may enter one track only. Indie Developer permits individuals, students and teams of up to four. Startup is for technical founders building a product; its representative must be an authorized active employee. The retrieved rules state no startup team-size cap.
- A team representative submits, identifies members and must be authorized to act for the team. Consult the full official rules for the complete restrictions and agreements.

## Project themes and rubric

The themes are **Build for Your World**, solving a problem in a known domain, and **Push the Frontier**, enabling new capabilities or real-world scientific and physical-system work.

| Project criterion | Weight | Evidence to show |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Impact | 30% | Who benefits, how much the problem matters, potential real use and fit to a theme/problem statement. |
| Use of Meta AI Tooling | 25% | Creative use beyond a basic integration and useful unexpected capabilities. |
| Technical Depth & Execution | 20% | Sound, thoughtfully refined engineering and craft. |
| Demo | 25% | A working impressive demo, live robustness and demo-video quality. |

Application selection uses separate, equally weighted project-idea and technical-skill criteria. The project rubric above comes from the official rules.

## Models, work and submission

The rules say participants build with Meta AI products, including Muse Spark, Glimmer, SAM 3 and other products identified on the event page. The current event page lists Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, SAM 3.1, Meta open weights models and Muse Code. It does not require every named model or the coding agent. Meta-tooling use accounts for 25% of project judging. This does not establish that the app's configured provider route or returned model output works live.

The rules name [Cerebral Valley's submission portal](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit). It was not opened in this check. A working demo and demo-video quality are judged, but the retrieved event page and rules give **no complete artifact checklist, video-duration limit, source/public-repository requirement, hosted-URL requirement or portal field list**. Check the participant portal and participant communications before finalizing those artifacts. The proposed demo length in this repository is an editing assumption.

Submissions must be original work owned by the entrant/team/organization and must respect others' IP, proprietary and privacy rights. Open-source components are allowed when licenses are followed and the project enhances or builds on the underlying product. **No explicit pre-existing-work ban, fresh-repository rule or required work-start date was found in these two documents.** Their absence does not establish blanket permission for prior work; resolve any project-specific uncertainty before submission.

Entrants retain ownership, while the rules grant organizers broad perpetual rights to use, adapt and publish submissions and say event materials are not confidential. Verify footage, components and intended submission use against actual permission records. This document does not establish asset rights or learning outcomes.
